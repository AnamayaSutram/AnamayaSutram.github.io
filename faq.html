Live Sessions

Sessions are conducted live and one-to-one via Zoom. You receive real-time guidance, personalised movement adaptations, and careful corrections based on how your body responds throughout the session.

The programme includes three live one-to-one sessions each week:

  • Two guided sessions of 30 minutes each
  • One 20-minute practice session, followed by 15 minutes of weekly monitoring and interactive discussion

The interactive period is used to review your progress, discuss your experience, address difficulties, and adapt your upcoming practice when required.

If you have joined primarily to maintain general fitness and wellbeing, you may use this time, at your discretion, for questions, clarification, or further guidance related to your practice.

Attendance, Rescheduling and Programme Validity

You may reschedule up to two sessions during the 12-week programme, provided advance notice is given.

Further missed sessions, late cancellations, or no-shows will be treated as completed sessions and cannot be carried forward. Genuine emergencies may be considered individually.

If Anāmaya Sūtram cancels a session, it will be rescheduled and will not count towards your two permitted reschedules.

Sandhi Prāṇa is designed as a 12-week programme. Up to two eligible missed sessions may be completed within an additional 15-day completion period.

Any unused sessions remaining after this period will lapse and cannot be carried forward, transferred, or refunded. Exceptional circumstances may be considered individually.

Yes. You may request one programme pause of up to 14 calendar days due to illness, unavoidable travel, or a genuine emergency. Advance notice should be provided wherever possible.

After you restart, the same session timing will be retained where possible; however, its availability cannot be guaranteed.

Longer interruptions arising from exceptional medical or family circumstances may be considered individually. Unused sessions cannot be transferred or extended indefinitely.

If a session is significantly interrupted by a genuine technical or internet-connectivity problem, the circumstances will be considered individually.

Where appropriate, the remaining session time may be rescheduled, subject to availability. Genuine technical interruptions will not automatically be counted among your two permitted rescheduled sessions.

Payments and Refunds

Once your consultation is complete and the programme is confirmed as suitable for you, payment details will be shared directly.

Payment can be made through the available UPI or bank-transfer options. Your enrolment will be confirmed after the payment has been received.

Any available instalment option may be discussed before enrolment.

Once agreed, the payment schedule must be followed within the specified timelines to ensure uninterrupted participation in the programme.

A refund request may be submitted within two calendar days of payment, provided the programme has not started.

After two calendar days—or once the first session has been conducted—the programme fee becomes non-refundable and non-transferable. Duplicate or incorrect payments will be reviewed separately.

Completing the Programme

Towards the end of the programme, your progress, present needs, and future goals will be reviewed.

Where continued guidance may be beneficial, suitable continuation options will be discussed individually. Any continuation will be optional, separately charged, and subject to suitability and availability.

Anāmaya Sūtram and Its Illustrations

Anāmaya Sūtram grew from the founder’s personal experience of navigating hormonal imbalance, obstructive sleep apnoea, acidity and IBS, a lung infection, excess weight, and increasing joint-related difficulties.

These challenges affected her sleep, energy, mobility, and everyday life. Her search for a more informed and sustainable approach to wellbeing led her towards breathwork, adapted movement, yoga wellness, and mudras—eventually transforming a personal journey into a purpose to support others.
